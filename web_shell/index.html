Bishop Web Shell

This page verifies that Bishop-owned WASM can drive browser rendering.

Click or Tab to focus the canvas. Use arrows or WASD; move and click the mouse inside it. Left-click the focused canvas to hear a short tone.

In the playable-fixture export, wait for “fixture ready,” then left-click to start, reach the goal, and press R to reset. This fixture uses Rust-directed fallback canvas drawing, not the desktop renderer.
